Issues and resolutions

Every open decision and known defect, and how each earlier one was resolved. Click an entry to expand it.

Last checked: 2026-11-08, against phase1/planar at 6af5dd3 in Symplectomorphism/c-gnn. The full records are in the discussion compendium (reports/discussion-compendium/); commits are on that branch.

Open

ImportantThe per-pose and sampled gates don’t check the disk’s clearance from the environment (open)

Their transition sweep never checks that the disk stays clear of the environment, so on one formation they admit making disk contact with the disk inside the platform. The relaxed gate (now the default) is unaffected. Being fixed; it changes those gates’ counts. Node identity.

ImportantThe hard break(gv) starts: solved by a learned skill, not yet used by the search (open)

The quasi-static realizers fail the hard break(gv) starts: with friction-matched plans the disk’s contact stays inside its cone, but the box’s corners on the floor and riser slide. A learned SAC skill now solves the family (18/18 hard, 150/150 held-out); open: how the search should call it, and whether the strict test should require the box to settle (the skill holds the new contacts in a small limit cycle). The local solver.

WarningTies under deep overlap (εpen = 5 mm is provisional)

εpen is ten times εon, so both corners of a tilted box edge can count as touching. 475 of 4,000 demo poses jittered by about 3 mm are ambiguous (474 with a contact deeper than εon; in 136, sv loses to t, which contains it). A tied pose is never a search node or a solver success, so no wrong label is used; the stress test’s budget is 500. Since step 3b εpen = 5 mm (257 ties); 2–3 mm (44–98 ties) needs stiffer contacts, which keep penetration under 0.22 mm but need the controller retuned first. Contact states.

WarningThe search: success flag ignored, requested formation stored, breaks unchecked, no goal

Angelo’s Search accepts when the requested contacts are in band, ignoring the solver’s success flag; a node stores the requested formation, not the one reached (28 of 30 nodes in a 12-round run); a break is accepted without checking that the contact left (6 of 7); there is no goal test, goal input or budget. Fix: wiring Λ’s shared success test into the search, then the goal-directed search of step 4. Walkthrough 8. Nov 8, fix in progress (7939366, Decision 18): a call counts only if Λ succeeds and the recognizer returns exactly the requested formation; the child stores the recognized formation; the approach must stay in σ. Moves to Resolved when the full test runs pass.

WarningThe hold tests are float32

The gate agrees with a float64 reference on 1,000 of 1,000 random cases and on every near-boundary case 0.33 mm or more from the boundary. Its only misses are acceptances with margin 0 (at most 2·10−7) at the boundary pose itself. A positive acceptance threshold would refuse them (choice 4 above). The hold gate.

WarningThe LP gate in 3D needs reinversion

Float32 rounding builds up in linrax’s dense tableau over the long pivot sequences of 3D cones: about 1% of supported 3D cases end “uncertain” (rejected, never certified wrongly). On the GPU the 3D test sees 4 uncertain of 64 against its budget of 2, so test_3d_cones_finish_and_never_mislead fails there; it passes on the CPU. Fix: rebuild the tableau from the final basis and continue, with the 3D build. Background.

NoteSmaller open items
  • Label flicker near θe along a trajectory (frames 623–625 of the demo). Fix: hysteresis with thresholds from a noise model, in the execution monitor.
  • GPU runs are not repeatable between processes by default (one MPPI call ended flush once and tipped 1.4° once). Workaround: XLA_FLAGS=--xla_gpu_deterministic_ops=true, about 13% slower.
  • The 3D catalog (step.yaml) keeps the old defects (closed faces over-count, ±y faces, a Python loop over templates). Frozen with 3D.
  • ContactGraph drops edges (self.edges never set). Nothing uses the graph; superseded by attention over templates.
  • tests/test_local_solver.py reads gradient_warm_start, which MPPI no longer has. For a fix-up PR to Angelo.
  • The paper’s pending results: step-3 choices and results, the scene families and the ranker are marked pending in the rewritten paper.

Resolved

TipThe disk is collapsed: the relaxed gate is the default (Nov 8, Decision 21, 72bb943)

The gate now asks whether some disk position in the disk’s region holds the box through an edit (one LP over the reachable stretch of the face), so two states differing only in where the disk sits are one node. Measured against the per-pose and sampled gates: Λ accepts 0.36 of its calls (0.27, 0.26), and 119 of 370 spread-out start–edit pairs succeed (45, 60), at 1.3× the per-pose cost. Node identity.

TipThe hard break(gv) is solved by a learned skill (Nov 8, 9e7211f)

SAC trained from scratch in the MJX simulator solves 18/18 hard and 150/150 held-out starts under the strict test, where Λqs and MPPI solve almost none. Not yet wired into the search. The local solver.

TipCluster jobs died with “LLVM: Cannot allocate memory” (Nov 8, 1e6fce8)

Juno and Ganymede2 cap a process at 65,530 memory mappings (this workstation allows 1,048,576). The gate ran outside jit, so XLA compiled one module per operation and shape: seven gate calls in one test added 78,000 mappings. With the gate jitted inside Cand, they add 11,000; the full test suite on Juno went from about 10 to 3.6 minutes, with no failures. A guard (cmg.mapguard) clears JAX’s caches if a long process nears the limit.

TipThe graph W is a neighbor graph; merging by evaluation (Nov 8, Decision 19)

Single changes now need the two formations to be realized within 25 mm of each other (as compound edges already did), formations must be recognizable, and vertex–vertex contacts appear only alone. W has 328 formations, 2,398 single and 92 compound edges; the pivot stays compound. Certified constants gave a merge radius under 1 mm, so duplicates are merged only when the gate gives equal verdicts (within a 10 mm test radius). Node identity.

TipNode identity: from a rounded pose to formation + disk region + box pose (Nov 8, Decisions 17 and 18)

The search keyed nodes by their formation and a pose rounded to 1 mm / 0.01 rad cells, a placeholder from the first search stub that Decision 9 (adopting Xiao and Ji) never revisited. The paper noticed the gap and recorded it instead of raising it; an audit then found nine more unrecorded divergences from Xiao and Ji, decided in Decision 18. Xiao and Ji’s contact state (formation + connected region) assumes a driven object; our box moves only when the disk pushes it, so the key collapses only the disk’s region and keeps the box’s pose, merged within a margin-certified radius. Nothing is snapped. Explained with worked examples on Node identity.

NoteStep 3d choices decided; the sweep is now adaptive (Nov 8, Decision 16, 84b7ab2)

The friction cap stays unset. The sweep’s resolution was measured on 1,797 candidates against a dense reference: at the simulator’s friction every grid agrees; at μ = 0.35 the old 5-point grid refuses one realizable break, 9 points and the new adaptive grid (64 poses on the free directions, two refinement levels, 118 poses) none. The adaptive grid is the step scene’s default; it costs what the old grid cost. The sweep’s resolution.

TipThe gate’s sweep probed different poses on the CPU and the GPU (Nov 8, f54dc73)

The transition sweep’s grid followed the SVD’s null vectors, which are any rotation of a basis when two or more directions are free; the CPU and the GPU returned different rotations. Harmless at μ = 0.99, decisive at 0.35. Now a canonical basis of the subspace (pivoted Gram–Schmidt on its projector); the backends agree, and a test rotates the basis and checks nothing changes.

TipStep-3c choices decided: open loop stays the default, RH20 optional, F1/F2 removed (Nov 8, Decision 15)

Aykut accepted the recommendations. Λqs open loop stays the default realizer; receding-horizon re-planning stays available with RH20 and χtr kept fixed as its defaults (half the tracking deviation, no gain in success); F1 and F2 are removed from the code (± 1 run, opposite signs on GPU and CPU). Next: the friction match (step 3d, below and on the local solver); then a tracker that can use high friction; quasi-dynamic methods last.

TipThe GPU’s −96 mm gap was a float32 sliver in the penetration attribution (Nov 8)

At the landed pose with the box’s top face almost parallel to the riser, the box’s lower corner sits 2·10⁻⁸ m inside the platform (float32 noise). That penetration belongs to the platform, the nearer segment, but on the GPU a one-ulp piece of the face, λ ∈ [0.99999994, 1], stayed attributed to the riser: it has no representable midpoint, so the strict “another segment is closer” test was evaluated at its end and failed, and the sliver carried the riser’s h = −96 mm as its depth. Fix: attributed pieces narrower than 10⁻⁶ (in λ) are dropped; CPU and GPU now agree (+96.24 mm), and the pose is a test (test_gap_sign_near_parallel_resting_box).

TipExact validity and the local compound rule (Nov 8, Decisions 11, 12, 14; merge 9ea09bb)

Validity now comes from the geometry: a certified table of the 357 formations some admissible pose realizes (the stand-in admitted 37,203), from 552 excluded pairs and 4 triples. Compound edges follow the local rule by default (Decision 14): no intermediate is realized within ρ = 25 mm of the transition pose. On the step scene that gives 96 compound instances (the global rule 136; the dropped 40 join formations realized 85–184 mm apart, which no single motion connects); the pivot stays compound. The global rule stays selectable. Edits.

TipDuplicate keys: safe pruning and canonical states, so the call bound holds (Nov 8, Decision 13)

The theory note proves that the search makes at most min ν/π calls of Λ (Orseau and Lelis’s policy-guided search bound). With duplicate node keys the bound needs two things: nodes of one key behave alike (one canonical state per key, the first one made) and the safe rule: drop a new child only if a node already made with its key has π at least as large; otherwise make it as a new node. First-come pruning breaks the bound (17,664 calls against 10,020 in the theory note’s example); so does re-parenting the existing node to the higher-π arrival (26 against 22.2 in a small example). Implemented in cmg/search (SearchState, PolicyRanker) with both examples as tests. The canonical states were later withdrawn: with the node key of Decision 17 every node keeps its real state; the safe rule stays.

TipRe-planning restarted the change at every re-plan (Nov 8, 7e2234f)

The first re-planning version gave every new plan the time the open-loop plan had left, re-swept χtr, and re-planned phase I while the box was still recognized as σ, which it is until the change completes. The change was pushed to the last steps and made fast: make(s) and transfer(sv → t) failed by overshoot, and re-planning was worse than open loop. Fixed: each phase keeps its scheduled end, χtr is kept, and once the plan has passed χtr the previous plan continues until σ′ is recognized. The local solver.

TipStep-3 choices decided: the mode-scheduled realizer, cost E, εpen = 5 mm, thresholds (Nov 7, 767ab7a, 6dec516)

Aykut chose a mode-scheduled quasi-static realizer with tracking in MuJoCo (Λqs) over MPPI, which stays as the baseline with cost E (the paper’s terms over εon); εpen = 5 mm; χtr the nearest swept pose with margin ≥ γtr = 0.1; acceptance at margin ≥ γmin = 10⁻⁵; the sweep’s defaults. Λqs realizes every chain edit from its canonical start, including the pivot and break(gv) at 45°, which no MPPI cost realized (checkpoint 2: 23 of 25, against 13 of 25 for MPPI-E). The local solver.

TipXiao and Ji’s construction adopted (Nov 7, 9261ece)

Our construction had drifted from Xiao and Ji’s contact formations in ways that were conveniences, not needs. A literature search (13 papers read in full) found nothing simpler that still meets our needs: stances, body-pair strata and scene graphs cannot see a box tip from its edge onto its corner; configuration-space cells and triangulated subcontacts split one motion into several edges. We now follow Xiao and Ji exactly and add only tolerances, several bodies with a manipulator, and a no-contact state per pair.

The landed box became {t} (next entry). Contact states, edits.

TipThe landed box is {t}, not {sv, t} (Nov 7, accepted by Aykut)

Once +xB lies on the platform, vS bounds the touching platform edge, so sv is not principal. Landing is transfer(sv → t), sliding off the corner is transport, and the chain is one edit shorter. Support is unchanged. Edits.

TipTransfers, the disk around a corner, corner ownership (Nov 7, 9261ece)
  • 44 of the 92 old transfers were not Xiao–Ji neighbors: 20 went round a corner in one step, 24 changed both features at once. Transfers are now one-sided containment only (56).
  • The disk goes round a box corner in two transfers, through its contact with the vertex.
  • No ownership rule is needed: the environment is one object whose convex vertices come from its geometry (this replaces the Nov 5 rule that the riser owns the step corner).
TipCompound edges: the two-transfer rule, then the general rule (Nov 7, 5b25385, 9261ece)

The pivot needs g → gv and s → sv together, since both one-at-a-time intermediates are excluded. Settled first as a closed form (two transfers in different body pairs with crossed exclusions), it was replaced the same day by Xiao and Ji’s neighbor relation with the minimality rule, which gives the same edges on the step and needs no special case.

TipThe gate tests the less-constrained end; a transfer keeps the old normal (Nov 7, a3789d7, 9261ece)

H first tested the contacts that persist through an edit, with the old contact’s normal for a transfer (on the demo the two normals differ by at most 1.77° where it matters; using the new one changes none of 1,155 decisions). Under Xiao and Ji’s containment both follow from one rule: test the edit’s less-constrained end, as multi-contact planners do (Hauser et al. 2005, 2008; Escande et al. 2013); the normal comes from the feature that stays.

TipThe margin and the gate’s solver (Nov 7, 228950f)

The margin is set inclusion of a disturbance polytope, by default the ℓ₁ ball (0.2 = μ on the flat box). The disk’s force is bounded by its motors (±1 N, read from the MuJoCo model), which also made margins with the disk finite; before, its unbounded push made the support cone fill all of 3D. The LP backbone is the gate; the exact cone test is its cross-check. The hold gate.

TipThe gate at χ₀ refused realizable edits (Nov 7, 41b5721, 26c96d0)

break(gv) was refused at 30° although tilting further and then lifting is possible. H now searches near χ₀ over the more-constrained end’s contacts for a transition pose where the less-constrained end holds, and passes it to the solver. Pose distances use the paper’s weight a/2 on angles. The hold gate.

TipOne success test for every realizer (Nov 7, 41b5721)

MPPI graded itself with its own gap-band test. Every realizer now uses the paper’s success test, extended to transfers and compound edges. The local solver.

TipThe paper rewritten on the current construction (Nov 7, e2f705c to 63de648)

The old draft (archived, tag paper-v1-2026-11-07) described corner ownership, one contact per body pair, the old chain and stale numbers for h = a/4. The paper was rewritten from scratch and revised after four independent reviews; the proofs that the gate never refuses a realizable edit and that the recognizer is correct are in its appendices.

TipThe hold LPs in float32 with linrax (Nov 6, e29d96f, b354251)
  • linrax had no iteration cap and could cycle on degenerate 3D LPs, hanging a whole vmap batch: its pivot loop is replaced (300 pivots per phase, Bland’s leaving rule, artificial rows marked correctly).
  • It returned infeasible points as optimal in float32: fixed by Householder-spread rows, primal and dual from the final basis, pivot tolerance 10−4, exit cap 10, and every answer checked.
  • linrax has no license file; accepted (Aykut, Nov 6), pinned to 0.3.0.
TipStep 2’s foundations (Nov 5, d810e32, 1986c73)
  • Cand had no validity check (a second contact per pair, excluded formations, transport always legal): edits now pass validity, transfer and compound rules; transport only when supported.
  • No transfer generator: g → gv needed a break and a make through a formation where the box does not touch the floor.
  • MPAX’s “not supported” was uncertified (192–194 of 416 supported near-boundary cases rejected): replaced by the exact cone test with witness and certificate, later by the LP gate.
  • The cone test’s margin was positive on a single-ray cone and failed below three generators: fixed, and the margin proved to be the signed distance.
  • JAX’s reduced-precision (TF32) GPU products broke the supporting-plane checks: dot products are formed elementwise.
  • Tied poses are treated as ambiguous: never a node or a solver success.

TipThe edge-contact tilt θe (Nov 5, 5f5a2e8)

A length rule (“flat if 7.8 mm lie within 1 mm”) called a box tipped 7.35° flat, and as the box tipped over the step corner it switched the floor contact at 7.35° but the riser contact at 8.44°, labeling the impossible {gv, s} in between. An angle shared by both contacts fixes this. Two principled options were compared: the engines’ point rule (both ends of the overlap within 1 mm), which switches floor and riser at 0.46° and 1.83° and realizes {gv, s} on 13 demo frames; and one derived angle, θe = arcsin(εon/h) ≈ 1.83°, which switches both together and is exactly the point rule on the riser. The derived angle was adopted (a 2° value tuned to the demo, 62782b8, was withdrawn). Hysteresis for trajectory labels was deferred to the execution monitor.

TipStep 1’s foundations (Nov 4–5, ca4123b, b2665cb, 583810b)
  • One physical contact lit 3 to 6 template bits (closed features share boundaries): replaced by the principal-contact recognizer.
  • The hold test used one tie-broken point per contact, blended by smoothing, and ±y faces on a planar body: replaced by the contact sets’ endpoints in a planar catalog.
  • Exclusions were integer indices, then dropped; there were no vertex features for gv, sv: named templates and vertices in step2d.yaml.
  • Overlaps were charged to the wrong surface (−31 mm into the platform): each overlap is charged to the nearest environment segment or endpoint (approved Nov 5).
  • A Python loop over templates: one vmap per kernel (the 3D catalog still loops).
  • NaN gradients in the planar kernels: safe norms and roots.
  • The tie-break was kept as the recognizer’s fallback, with every use counted.
  • pyproject.toml was invalid TOML: fixed upstream.
