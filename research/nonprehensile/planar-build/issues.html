Issues

The open work and known defects, and the decisions behind the current design. Click an entry to expand it.

Last checked: 2026-11-13, against master at 49aaf57 in Symplectomorphism/c-gnn. The full records are in the discussion compendium (reports/supporting_docs/discussion-compendium/).

Open

ImportantFinding the indirect lift routinely (open; the target)

The indirect lift, held at every instant, is the paper’s running chain; its last four edits and closing transport are certified over μ ∈ [0.3, 0.5]. From the scene alone, the planner finds the held indirect lift in 193 of 200 held-out searches (96.5%, Wilson 93–98%; mean 89 calls to a solve) and in 33 of 34 on the original test set, with V₁ and π of round 1 (192 calls each), in the current configuration: admissible transition poses, δ-eroded transport targets, the box’s rotation over ±180°, EULERDAMP disabled (MJX integrates the solved acceleration) (Results). Of the 7 failures among the 200, 6 run out of actions to try and one reaches the budget; the original set’s one failure (seed 11, start 14, μ = 0.3) reaches the budget. With EULERDAMP on (192 of 200, 33 of 34), 8 earlier failures are now solved and 7 earlier solves are not (p = 1); three of those failures came from MJX’s kicks. Round 2 of V₁ and π (408 training searches) ranks better offline but solves exactly the same searches as round 1 (193 of 200, 33 of 34, p = 1): the failures are exhausted candidate sets, not ordering. V₀ alone is not re-measured in this configuration; in the previous one (±90° stop, no erosion) it solved 17 of 34 and V₁ + π 26. Open: the remaining failures; the short searches (in the previous configuration none of 18 solved with 64 calls: the roll from the tilts first reached is refused); and the next rounds of V₁ and π (training searches that reach the goal, exploration). Given the formation sequence as a hint, the same planner solves only at μ = 0.5: the hint removes the other routes.

ImportantNear-future: the disk’s motion keeps the box held; pushes aimed inside their targets

1. The disk’s motion keeps the box held (#23). Every disk motion, the approach included, must keep the box held under the gate’s test, including the disk’s own contact force. The diagnosed fall had a transition pose 16 mm inside the box: the approach drove the disk’s reference into it and the disk’s PD pressed harder and harder. Part 1 is on by default: transition poses must be admissible, so no disk target is buried in the box. On the 34 held-out searches falls drop by 17–23% and the solves are unchanged. Part B is built and measured: the approach covers the whole run to its target, the hold test runs along that path with the disk’s predicted force as an external load, and a failing approach is refused before anything moves. On the 34 held-out searches it solves the same 33 with the same calls; its 27 refusals fall where the box would have fallen (22 of the 25 that can be compared). It is on by default (cmg 0.1.1).

2. Pushes aimed inside their targets (#24). The failing searches did not need the disk re-placed on its face; its height plays no part. They end in a dead band about 23–35 mm from the riser, where neither make(s) nor a push is offered: a push counted as reaching its target when it ended within δ of the target set’s far edge. Fixed by the δ-erosion of transport targets, on by default: pushes aim inside the target set by δ. Measured on the 34 held-out searches in the previous configuration: V₀ 17 → 26 of 34, V₁+π 26 → 33 of 34. Under the current configuration V₁+π solves 33 of 34, and 193 of 200 on a larger held-out set (Results).

3. A dynamic realizer (#34). The architecture is adopted: a certified tube per edit carries the claim and a fixed-mode MPC tracks inside it. The dynamic realizer.

ImportantThe realizer plans held poses, not stick–slip plans

Λqs plans positions only and models forces as the gate’s hold test at each knot (both edges of every cone): a sequence of static holds, Theorem B’s level. With its two path fixes (disk_path_fix, roll_press_fix, on by default) the unhinted search finds the indirect lift (Results). The gate stays at the hold level. A stick–slip variant of the realizer is built (stick_slip, off): it realizes 9–12 fewer of 227 replayed calls per μ and its chain search fails at 0.3, so it stays off. The local solver. No executed motion is certified: Λqs tracks within 8.1° and 17 mm at 95/95, against a certificate resolution of 0.25° × 0.5 mm. The replacement, a realizer that plans the motion with its contact dynamics, is on The dynamic realizer.

ImportantThe open work, in order
  1. Discovery. Rebuild 𝒱₀ for the current gate; train V₁ by expert iteration with exploration (π mixing, Go-Explore restarts), with a certified Lipschitz bound before V₁ decides acceptance; then the attention ranker, trained on held solves.
  2. A value that only orders (H4): lexicographic value, edge lengths from measured calls, the ηmax sweep; a derived replacement for the progress threshold δ = 10 mm, which rests on the declared merge radius.
  3. Parametric certificate of the support LP. Certify a hold along a whole path, not at sampled poses: the transport gate (it replaces the five-pose check), the lazy target query (an exclusion certificate) and the realizer (held between plan knots). Queued with it: a polynomial (sum-of-squares) certificate for large regions and an online tube certificate of the next segment of Λqs’s plan.
  4. Certified execution: the dynamic realizer (the A-tracker, then the B-tube, then the A3 check), with H2’s items: plan knots held throughout, admissible end knots, a tracking bound, the free-approach window; the pushes that stop 3–5 mm short (slide_lag stays until then).
  5. H3, a judge that measures “held”: the hold margin along the trajectory instead of the 6 mm kinetic-energy threshold for dynamic events.
  6. A3, the uncertainty model: ε by a 95/95 bound, the μ band’s robustness, εpen.
  7. Gate decisions (Aykut): the target threshold (R4); when an edit must close locally; the lazy query’s exclusion certificate; a gate whose necessity needs no Lipschitz or covering hypothesis (refuse only where the interval certificate proves “not held”); the recognizer rethink.
  8. Releases R1–R3: let go only at a held node, the physically possible outcomes, a statistical release certificate. A proposal; releases are off until then.
  9. Scenes: a taller step (H = a/2), the scene families, then a second disk.
  10. Housekeeping: JAX loops (the gate takes 20–35 s per call on GPU); the clusters’ memory-map limit (below).

WarningTies under deep overlap (εpen = 5 mm is provisional)

εpen is ten times εon, so both corners of a tilted box edge can count as touching. 257 of 4,000 demo poses jittered by about 3 mm are ambiguous at 5 mm (475 at 10 mm; in many, sv loses to t, which contains it). A tied pose is never a search node or a solver success, so no wrong label is used. 2–3 mm (44–98 ties) needs stiffer contacts, which keep penetration under 0.22 mm but need the controller retuned first. Contact states.

NoteSmaller open items
  • Lift searches exceed the clusters’ memory-map limit (#27) of 65,530 maps per process. One cut leaves results bit-identical and lowers the peak from about 100k to 79k maps, still above the limit; an HPC ticket to raise the limit is open.
  • Label flicker near θe along a trajectory. Fix: hysteresis with thresholds from a noise model, in the execution monitor.
  • GPU runs are not repeatable between processes by default. Workaround: XLA_FLAGS=--xla_gpu_deterministic_ops=true, about 13% slower.
  • 3D is frozen. The 3D hold LP needs reinversion (float32 rounding in linrax’s dense tableau leaves about 1% of supported 3D cases “uncertain”, never certified wrongly), and the 3D catalog (step.yaml) keeps its old defects. The project is planar.
  • ContactGraph drops edges (self.edges never set). Nothing uses the graph.
  • tests/test_local_solver.py reads gradient_warm_start, which MPPI no longer has. For a fix-up PR to Angelo.

Decisions behind the current design

TipSettled: the stuttering motion, replays across processors, the box’s rotation range
  • The stuttering motion (#25, closed). The renderer’s playback is fixed: one continuous timeline, with no frozen holds between calls. The chatter during the pivot is a MuJoCo sliding-contact artifact: above a slip speed v*(N) a loaded sliding contact floats to zero penetration, drops out for a step and the box bounces. v* is calibrated per contact pair, and its slope follows from the contact parameters (solref, solimp). Timing the plan below v* needs a Coulomb force solution, and Λqs’s hand-built disk path has none on the steep pivots, so the fix moves to the dynamic realizer, which chooses the disk’s path with its forces.
  • Replays across processors (#28, closed). Every run records its build (processor, instruction set, libraries, XLA settings) and its code commit. A replay on the same build must reproduce the run bit for bit; on another processor the difference is reported, not judged. Measured on 80 stratified calls: 80 of 80 bit-identical on one machine, 0 of 80 across a Ryzen and an i5, and 2 of 80 flip between success and failure.
  • The box’s rotation range is ±180°. The rotation joint was limited to ±90°, and that stop acted as a hidden support; it is relaxed to ±180°.
TipThe construction, the gate, the realizer and the search
  • Xiao and Ji’s contact formations, exactly, with tolerances, several bodies with a manipulator, and a no-contact state per pair: principal contacts, transfers by one-sided containment, compound edges by the minimality rule, the environment one polygon whose convex vertices come from its geometry. Simpler representations (stances, body-pair strata, scene graphs) cannot see a box tip from its edge onto its corner. Contact states, edits.
  • The landed box is {t}: landing is transfer(sv → t), and sliding off the corner is transport. Edits.
  • One derived edge-contact tilt, θe = arcsin(εon/h), shared by the floor and riser contacts. Contact states.
  • Validity from the geometry (a certified table of valid formations) and the local compound rule (ρ = 25 mm). Contact states, edits.
  • The gate tests the edit’s less-constrained end, with the disk’s motor limits, and its margin is a certified bound: “held” needs a witness, “not held” a Farkas certificate, else uncertain. It certifies holds only; stick or slip belongs to the realizer’s plan. The hold gate.
  • The transition sweep uses a canonical basis of the free directions and an adaptive grid (64 poses, two refinement levels); makes and constraining transfers close the added contact. The hold gate.
  • Disk friction is a declared band, μdisk ∈ [0.3, 0.5], one model everywhere; the gate and the tracker use 0.3.
  • The gate is relaxed over the disk’s region, and nodes are keyed by formation, disk region and box pose, merged only when the gate’s verdicts agree, with the safe duplicate rule. Node identity.
  • One success test for every realizer. The local solver.
  • Λqs’s feed-forward is the minimum-norm holding force, and its six options are on by default. The local solver.
  • Releases and the learned skills are in the code, off: the method’s lift is held at every instant, and no edit of it needs a learned skill. The local solver, search.
