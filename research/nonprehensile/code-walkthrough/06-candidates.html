6. Candidate edits

From a node with formation σ at pose χ, Cand.legal_ops lists the edits the search may hand to the solver. There are 24 makes (set one bit), 24 breaks (clear one bit), and one transport (keep σ and move). An edit to σ′ is legal when

  • it changes the formation (σ′ ≠ σ),
  • Hold: the contacts that persist, σ ∩ σ′, pass Supported at χ (step 5), and
  • Near: every added template has gap < phi_gate = 0.125 at χ.

Transport is always legal. All 48 make/break checks run in one vmap.

python walkthrough/06_candidates.py
The script, 06_candidates.py (download) 
"""Step 6: candidate edits (Cand.legal_ops).

From a node's formation, every single-bit make and break is a candidate,
plus one transport (no change). A make or break is legal when it changes
the formation, the persisting contacts pass Supported (Hold), and every
added template is within phi_gate (Near). Print the legal edits at three
fixtures.
"""
import time

import jax
import jax.numpy as jnp
import numpy as np

from cmg.marking.candidate import Cand

from _common import fixtures, load_spec, on, template_names

spec = load_spec()
cand = Cand.from_spec(spec)
names = template_names(spec, cand.catalog)
n = len(names)
legal_ops = jax.jit(lambda q: cand.legal_ops(config=q))

for fixture in ("flat", "flat_stable_close", "toppled30_close"):
    qpos = jnp.array(fixtures()[fixture])
    start = time.perf_counter()
    ops, types = legal_ops(qpos)
    ops = np.asarray(ops)
    elapsed = time.perf_counter() - start
    formation = cand.catalog.band(config=qpos)
    print(f"== {fixture}  ({elapsed:.1f} s)")
    print("  formation:", on(formation, names))
    print("  make: ", on(ops[:n], names))
    print("  break:", on(ops[n:2 * n], names))
    print("  transport:", bool(ops[2 * n]))

The first call compiles (about 22 s); later calls are fast.

What to notice

  • Makes are cheap to admit. A make keeps every current contact, so its hold test is just “is the current formation supported”, and Near is a distance threshold. At flat 16 of the 19 unset bits pass.
  • Near admits geometric nonsense. B:zp-L (the box’s top face onto the floor) is legal at flat: its gap, 0.125, is just under phi_gate (the kernels subtract about 1e−6). So are the disk on the box’s ±y faces, which the disk cannot reach because it never moves in y. The solver must reject each of them, one expensive call at a time (steps 7 and 8).
  • Breaks are where Hold bites. At flat only 3 of the 5 floor bits may be cleared; see exercise 6.2.
  • No exclusion is checked, because Cand never consults the contact graph (step 4).
  • The formation is recomputed from the band when none is supplied, as here. Inside the search, a node’s stored formation is used instead.

Code to read

src/cmg/marking/candidate.py: Cand.legal_ops, Cand.near, Cand.hold (the keep-set is new_formation & old_formation, which is the persistent-contact rule of Decision 7, bit for bit), Cand._hold_and_new (the σ′ ≠ σ check), and Cand.apply_op (how an operation index becomes a formation).

Exercises

6.1 Why is making M:boundary-B:xp illegal at flat?

TipSolution

Near fails: its gap is 0.14687 (step 2), above phi_gate = 0.125. The disk would have to travel around the box to reach its front face.

6.2 At flat, why are exactly B:xn-L, B:yn-L and B:zn-L breakable, and not B:xp-L or B:yp-L?

TipSolution

Use the points the LP sees, from exercise 5.1: xp at (−0.0375, −0.0375), xn at (−0.1625, −0.0625), yp at (−0.15, 0.0625), and yn and zn both at (−0.15, −0.0625). The center of mass is at (−0.1, 0).

  • Clearing yn or zn removes nothing, since the other one sits at the same point. Clearing xn removes a point that is not on the binding edge. In all three cases the hull still contains the center of mass, 1.35 cm inside.
  • xp is the only point with x > −0.1. Without it every point has x ≤ −0.15, and the center of mass is 5 cm outside the hull.
  • yp is the only point with y > 0. Without it every point has y < 0, and the center of mass is 4.9 cm outside.

Which bits may be dropped is decided by which corner the closest-point routine picks and how the five nudges blend it, not by the physics.

6.3 At toppled30_close, why is breaking M:boundary-B:xn illegal, and why is making B:xn-L legal?

TipSolution

The break would leave only environment contacts, and those fail EnvStable there (step 5): the box would fall back. The make keeps every current contact, which is supported, and the box’s −x face is within 0.125 of the floor because the box is tilted only 30°. Hold says nothing about whether the make can be realized; that is the solver’s job.
