Results

From the scene alone, the planner finds the indirect lift — the box held at every instant — in 193 of 200 held-out searches (96.5%), with a learned value V₁ and ranker π.

The task

The box starts at rest on the floor and must end fully on the platform, at rest. The disk alone cannot carry it. The indirect lift tips the box onto the step corner, lets the disk walk round to the box’s raised underside, lifts the box off the floor and rolls it onto the platform, the box held at every instant:

{g} → {mx, g} → {mx, g, s} ⇒ {mx, gv, sv} → {gv, sv} → {mz, gv, sv} → {mz, sv} → {mz, t} → (transport) goal.

The planner finds it from the scene alone

The search is given the scene and the goal, nothing else: every edit the gate admits except releases, transports toward the gate’s targets, and outcome children (a call’s child is the state it reached). Every dynamic outcome is rejected, so every solve is held. The motions are the quasi-static realizer Λqs in MuJoCo. Both test sets are held out (never used for training) and run at the band’s two ends μ = 0.3 and 0.5, each search up to 192 calls: 100 random lift starts (F1 seed 13), 200 searches; and the original test set, the canonical start (the disk 10 cm from the box) and 16 random lift starts (F1 seed 11), 34 searches. The ordering is V₁ + the learned ranker π (round 1), with V₀’s measured edge lengths.

100 random starts (F1 seed 13) 94 / 100 99 / 100 193 / 200 (96.5%; 93–98%) 89 / 91

Every solve is held and is the indirect lift; some hold the box by its lower corner instead of its underside (16 of the 193, one of the 33). Of the 7 failures among the 200, 6 run out of actions to try and one reaches the 192-call budget. The canonical start takes 86 calls at μ = 0.3 (11 on the path) and 94 at μ = 0.5 (12). The original set’s one failure (seed 11, start 14, μ = 0.3) reaches the budget.

With the simulator corrected. These searches run with MuJoCo’s EULERDAMP flag disabled, so MJX integrates the solved acceleration. With it on, MJX integrated a force sum that an unconverged contact solve could turn into a kick of a box at rest. On the same machines, the earlier runs solved 192 of 200 and 33 of 34: 8 earlier failures are now solved and 7 earlier solves are not (exact McNemar p = 1). Replayed call by call, three of the earlier failures come from such a kick (one of them the first push, which flipped the box upside down), two earlier solves relied on the box’s creep at rest failing a call, and the others part at falls that end differently or at float-level differences that grow along the search.

Round 2 of V₁ and π, trained on 408 searches (seeds 20–29), ranks better offline (V₁’s Spearman correlation 0.87 → 0.93, π’s top-1 84% → 94%) but solved exactly the same searches as round 1 with EULERDAMP on: 192 of 200 and 33 of 34 on the same machines (exact McNemar p = 1), with 88 calls to solve against 89 (not re-measured with EULERDAMP off). The failures were exhausted candidate sets, not ordering failures: on these starts, ordering is saturated.

The configuration (the defaults of master):

  • transition poses must be admissible: no disk target buried in the box;
  • transports aim inside their target sets by δ = 10 mm (the δ-erosion);
  • the box’s rotation range is ±180° (a ±90° joint stop could carry load);
  • each approach is checked by the gate’s hold test with the disk’s own force, and refused before the disk moves if it would tip the box (the same 33 are solved with the same calls without it);
  • unhinted search, dynamic outcomes rejected, 192 calls;
  • EULERDAMP disabled: MJX integrates the solved acceleration;
  • V₁ and π of round 1, V₀ with edge lengths measured from logged calls.

V₀ is the weighted graph distance to the goal plus a pose term (how it is built); its edge lengths come from the calls of round 0’s training searches (a transfer succeeds 46% of the time, a break 69%). V₁ and π are learned by fitted value iteration and the LTS loss from unhinted searches on other starts (F1 seeds 20–22, never the test set), in two rounds: 80 searches of 64 calls, then 48 of 192 calls run with round 0’s V₁ and π. V₁ enters only the search’s order; acceptance stays with V₀.

In both settings the learned pair solves every search V₀ solves and more (exact McNemar p = 0.004 without erosion, 0.016 with it), with 42–50% fewer calls where both solve. Without erosion, of the 8 searches round 1 left unsolved, 7 ran out of actions to try; with 64 calls none of 18 was solved: the search reached {mz, sv} with the box at 51–57°, where Λqs refuses the roll, and the longer searches found a re-grip (break mz to 63°, make mz).

With the formation sequence given

In the previous configuration (with EULERDAMP on), given the indirect lift’s sequence as a hint (at a node in the chain’s k-th formation only edits into the (k+1)-th and transports), the planner with V₀ with equal edge lengths and the uniform ranker solves at μ = 0.5 (15 calls, 12 on the path) and exhausts its search at μ = 0.3 and 0.4: the branch that would succeed depends on where a push ends to within a degree (break mx from 43.1° succeeds, from 43.8° it does not), and the hint leaves no other way. With V₀’s measured edge lengths, the hinted search solves at μ = 0.3, 0.4 and 0.5 (15, 13 and 15 calls), on the routes of the paper. What this shows, and what it does not

  • The lift, found and held, at both ends of the friction band, from the scene and the goal alone, on starts the learned parts never saw.
  • One scene, one task. The step and the box; other scenes (the families) are not run.
  • Budget. 7 of 200 searches (and 1 of 34) fail within 192 calls; 6 of the 8 run out of actions to try.
  • The realizer. Λqs plans held poses, not stick–slip plans (the local solver).

Configuration and reproduction

The searches ran at c-gnn commit 1917e2e (master with EULERDAMP disabled in the scene files), on Juno’s compute nodes; results and the comparison with the earlier runs: cmg/scripts/step4/chain_search/results/noeulerdamp. V₀: cmg/scripts/step4/value/results/v0_it1. V₁ and π: cmg/scripts/step4/chain_search/results/night_2026-11-13/model_v1pi_it1. Searches run on CPU, one per 3 cores; a search needs about 80–90 thousand memory mappings (CMG_MAPGUARD must allow them). All 234 searches ran on Juno’s compute nodes (AMD EPYC 9334, 262,144 mappings per process). From cmg/:

CMG_DISK_FRICTION=0.3 CMG_MAPGUARD=0 JAX_PLATFORMS=cpu PYTHONPATH=src .venv314/bin/python \
    scripts/step4/chain_search/run_chain.py OUT --no-hint --dynamic-outcomes reject --budget 192 \
    --v0 scripts/step4/value/results/v0_it1/v0_it1.json \
    --v1 scripts/step4/chain_search/results/night_2026-11-13/model_v1pi_it1 \
    --learned-pi scripts/step4/chain_search/results/night_2026-11-13/model_v1pi_it1 \
    --seed 11 --index 12
#   without --seed/--index: the canonical start; CMG_DISK_FRICTION=0.5: the band's other end
#   admissible transition poses, the delta-erosion and the approach check are on by default
